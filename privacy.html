Brick Sweep

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

In short

Brick Sweep doesn't collect any data about you. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game has no account or server of its own.

On your device

Your saved game, scores, settings, challenges and achievement progress are kept on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center, which handles them under Apple's privacy policy. Each score carries a short summary of its run (how long it lasted, the level, its Super Sweeps and the rough shape of its points), which other players see when they tap it on a board. The game shows your Game Center name on challenges you share.

Sharing

Score cards and challenge links go only where you send them, through the iOS share sheet. A challenge link carries the run, your Game Center name and any name you gave the challenge. Saving a score card to Photos asks for your permission first.

Children

Brick Sweep collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear in the app (menu ▸ ⓘ) and on this page before it takes effect.
